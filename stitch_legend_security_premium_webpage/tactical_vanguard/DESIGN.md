## Brand & Style

This design system expresses absolute authority, elite protection, and tactical discipline. Designed for an ultra-premium private security and executive bouncer firm, the UI projects unwavering reliability, physical dominance, and technological sophistication. The aesthetic blends tactical operations telemetry with executive luxury.

The visual style is **High-Contrast Tactical Precision**:
- Surface depth built on layered shades of obsidian, carbon, and pitch black.
- Hyper-focused visual hierarchy where tactical crimson and alert red pull the eye directly to vital intelligence, instant deployment controls, and threat monitoring states.
- Clean structural engineering inspired by command control dashboards, heads-up displays (HUD), and fortified architectural spaces.
- Precise wire lines, micro crosshair markers, and telemetry corner notches that reinforce military-grade operational readiness.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high dark-to-light contrast, steel instrumentation, and calculated red alerts:

- **Dominant Base (`#090A0F` & `#0E1017`)**: Pitch black canvasses that simulate hardened tactical monitors and eliminate visual distraction.
- **Surface Elevation (`#161822`, `#1F2333`)**: Deep obsidian planes dedicated to telemetry modules, operative cards, and incident feeds.
- **Accents & Action Triggers (`#E50914`, `#DC2626`)**: Tactical crimson reserved strictly for high-priority actions, dispatches, emergency calls, authorization buttons, and status indicators.
- **Telemetry Cyan (`#38BDF8`)**: Secondary indicator reserved for active GPS tracks, secure comm channels, and authenticated encryption status.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#242838`, `#2E344A`)**: Low-reflectance steel strokes framing modules with clean edges.
- **Typography Scale**: Pristine optical white (`#FFFFFF`) for primary data and high-impact headers; cold muted steel (`#94A3B8`, `#64748B`) for operational labels, coordinates, and supporting metadata.

## Typography

The type system blends condensed, aggressive impact with surgical readability:

- **Display & Headlines (`Oswald`)**: Upright, condensed architecture reminiscent of military classification titles and command hierarchy. All primary headlines default to uppercase with slight letter-tracking (`+0.02em` to `+0.04em`) to project commanding presence.
- **Body (`Inter`)**: Neutral, neutral-contrast geometric sans-serif delivering razor-sharp legibility across dense mission briefings, personnel sheets, and incident debriefs.
- **Labels & Telemetry (`Space Grotesk`)**: Technical, monospace-leaning proportions with expanded tracking (`+0.06em` to `+0.12em`) used for mission clearance tiers, status tags, unit codes, timestamps, and callouts.

## Layout & Spacing

A mathematical 12-column rigid grid enforces disciplined vertical and horizontal alignment:

- **Grid Architecture**: 
  - **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 48px (`3rem`) outer safe margins.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns, 20px gutters, 32px (`2rem`) outer margins.
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 16px (`1rem`) outer margins.
- **Rhythm**: Baseline alignment calibrated in 4px / 8px increments. Tight grouping between metadata tags and primary labels, accompanied by breathing room around critical tactical command blocks.
- **HUD Reticle Dividers**: Grids utilize hairline border dividers (`1px solid #242838`) with hairline crosshair indicators (`+`) at major perimeter intersections to maintain tactical order.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **Tonal Surface Layering** and **Controlled Laser Luminescence** rather than traditional soft drop shadows:

1. **Base Surface (0dp)**: Jet black `#090A0F` canvas.
2. **Structural Panels (1dp)**: Obsidian `#0E1017` with hairline border `1px solid #242838`.
3. **Elevated Cards & Flyouts (2dp)**: Deep obsidian `#161822`, edged with `1px solid #2E344A`. Casts a subtle, tightly focused shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`.
4. **Active Crimson Overdrive**: High-priority interactive triggers, alert indicators, and dispatch states utilize crimson photonic bleeding:
   - Glow signature: `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(229, 9, 20, 0.35), inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.
5. **Tactical Backdrop Blurs**: Modals, drawer overlays, and floating status rails employ `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over `rgba(9, 10, 15, 0.88)` to retain background surveillance visibility.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Level 0 (Sharp)** philosophy:

- **0px Corner Radius**: Every card, input field, button, and banner features uncompromising 90-degree right angles, projecting structural reinforcement, armor-plating, and military-grade discipline.
- **Notched & Chamfered Motifs**: Key tactical components (such as primary dispatch CTAs, operative dossier tags, and status badges) leverage 45-degree chamfered top-right or alternating diagonal cutouts (`clip-path: polygon(...)`) to mimic armored composite plating and tactical hardware.
- **Shield Silhouette**: Reserved exclusively for official crest seals, agency verification insignias, and Tier-1 protection clearance certifications.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Dispatch CTA**: Full solid or linear-gradient red fill (`linear-gradient(135deg, #E50914 0%, #B91C1C 100%)`). 0px radius, sharp uppercase Oswald typography (`label-lg`), white text with `0 0 10px rgba(255,255,255,0.4)` text-shadow. Hover triggers an intensified outer red halo (`0 0 24px rgba(229, 9, 20, 0.6)`) and an inner white hairline rim.
- **Secondary / Tactical Line Button**: Transparent background, `1px solid #2E344A`, text `#94A3B8`. Hover converts border to `#FFFFFF`, shifts background to `#161822`, and text to `#FFFFFF`.
- **Chamfered Action Button**: Corner notch cutout (6px) on top-right, accompanied by a small micro-bracket glyph `[ SECURE NOW ]`.

### Cards & Telemetry Containers
- **Operative / Deployment Card**: Background `#161822`, perimeter border `1px solid #242838`. Top header bar features an obsidian strip `#0E1017` with micro status dot (crimson for deployed, emerald for standby, amber for transit). Hairline tactical crosses at corner vertices.

### Badges & Threat Indicators
- **High-Alert / Tier Tag**: Sharp rectangular badges with solid `#E50914` background or `#1F1014` dark fill bordered by `1px solid #E50914`. Space Grotesk uppercase lettering (`label-tactical`).
- **Security Clearance Seal**: Muted carbon badge (`#161822`) with metallic steel border (`#64748B`) and holographic silver sheen.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Tactical Field**: Background `#0E1017`, border `1px solid #242838`, text `#FFFFFF`. Focus transitions border to `#E50914` with a minimal outer ambient red aura (`0 0 8px rgba(229, 9, 20, 0.25)`). Placeholder styled in `#64748B`.
- **Radio Buttons & Checkboxes**: Square, 0px radius. Checked state fills with crimson `#E50914` featuring a crisp geometric white center pip or checkmark.

### Lists & Operative Rosters
- **Roster Line**: Row-level styling separated by hairline borders (`1px solid #161822`). Hover elevates row to `#161822` with a `2px solid #E50914` vertical indicator tab pinned to the left edge.
- **Data Telemetry Grid**: Paired key-value arrangements displaying time-stamped logs, client clearance levels, and location coordinates formatted in Space Grotesk uppercase.

### Specialized Agency Components
- **Shield Verification Stamp**: Vector emblem with sharp-angled shield outline containing stylized dual-diagonal crossbars and agency designation.
- **Incident Status Matrix**: Segmented 3-state emergency toggle (Defcon / Alpha-Bravo-Charlie status) using high-contrast colored LED-style glowing pills.
